Questions about billing

Do I pay for runners?
No. Runners are unlimited on every plan. They run on your machines, so they cost us nothing.
What about the agents' own usage?
Each agent's provider bills it as it does today, on your existing plan or API key. Tackora shows it per repository, person and day; it doesn't resell it.
Who counts as a member?
Anyone in the organization: they see the board, answer agents and can connect machines. Invite and remove people any time; billing follows the number of members.
Can I cancel?
Any time. The plan runs to the end of the month you've paid for, and you keep the Free plan's features after that.
