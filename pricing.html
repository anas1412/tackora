Questions about billing

What's a machine?
A computer connected with npx tackora start, where your agents run: a laptop, a desktop or a server. Its runner is free to install; the plan sets how many you connect.
What about the agents' own usage?
Each agent's provider bills it as it does today, on your existing plan or API key. Tackora shows it per repository, person and day; it doesn't resell it.
How does the Team trial work?
14 days of Team, no card needed, once per person. Start it in Settings, or when you invite someone or connect a machine over your plan's limit.
Can I cancel?
Any time. The plan runs to the end of the month or year you've paid for, then it's Free. Nothing is deleted: machines and repositories over Free's limits wait until you upgrade or remove some.
Need more than 5 people?
Write to us: bigger teams get a plan of their own.
Who handles payment?
Polar, our merchant of record: it takes the payment, handles sales tax and VAT, and sends the invoices. Tackora never sees your card.
