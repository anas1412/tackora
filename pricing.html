Questions about billing

Is Personal really free?
Yes, for good. It's your own organization, made when you sign up: just you, on as many machines and repositories as you like. It never asks for a card.
What makes something a team?
Another person. Your personal organization is only yours; to work with someone, create a team. The price is per team: two teams are two subscriptions, $49 each.
What about the agents' own usage?
Each agent's provider bills it as it does today, on your existing plan or API key. Tackora shows it per repository, person and day; it doesn't resell it.
Can I cancel?
Any time, in Manage billing. The plan runs to the end of the month or year you've paid for. After that the team's board stays, but it gets no work until it's paid for again.
More than 5 people?
Write to us: bigger teams get a plan of their own.
Who handles payment?
Polar, our merchant of record: it takes the payment, handles sales tax and VAT, and sends the invoices. Tackora never sees your card.
